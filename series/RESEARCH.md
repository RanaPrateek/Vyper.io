# Format research

## @followerscity "Mayor Town": days 2, 3 and 5 (studied 19 Nov 2026)
Same genre as us: a city built from followers, comments and votes. It grew from 0 to 9,199 residents in 2 days and 26,400 by day 5, posting **daily**. Episodes run 55–66 s.

### What they do that works
| Technique | How they do it | What we take (our own version) |
|---|---|---|
| **Growth number as the hook** | "Yesterday, 16,200. Today, 26,400." in the first 4 s, with a giant number on screen | Open every episode with the population sign counting from yesterday's followers to today's |
| **A character with personality** | The Mayor faints, "loses his mind", "is not happy", jokes about his ego | We need our own character. No mayor or top hat (that's theirs); see the ideas below |
| **Named shout-outs, granted literally** | "Someone asked for a cardboard box. So now they live in one." "Isabel, here's your farm. You can stop now." | Feature 4–6 named commenters per episode, each granted with a punchline. This is the main reason people comment |
| **Comment card on screen** | Their comment UI card with @username and likes shown over the build | We have one already: use it for every shout-out |
| **Results in suspense order** | Loser first, then runner-up, then the winner with a crown ("It lost. Again.") | Reveal results lowest to highest, and let a running joke build up (an option that keeps losing) |
| **Milestone unlock** | A sealed mystery building that "opens at 10,000"; a progress bar towards the next one | Our own milestone: e.g. **at 100 citizens the city gets electricity**, so the first night episode with lights. Show a progress bar |
| **Early-follower status** | "OG resident" ticket for following before 10k | Our own version: a **Founders' Wall** at the city entrance with the first citizens' names |
| **Pause-to-find-your-name** | The final screen is a wall of hundreds of usernames | End on a dense **citizen list** so people pause and rewatch to find their name (a strong retention signal) |
| **Daily rhythm** | "See you tomorrow" | Post more often (see schedule) |
| **Colour and crowds** | Saturated low-poly, rainbow roofs, crowds of tiny people everywhere | Warmer, more colourful palette for houses; little citizens walking around |
| **Text** | Two-line titles: a small label plus a big yellow-orange title with a dark outline, always inside the frame | Matches the account holder's feedback: smaller titles, always inside the safe area |
| **Narration** | Conversational and deadpan-funny, short punchy sentences, natural rhythm | Write jokes, not announcements, and generate the voice as continuous takes |

### Their episode structure (about 60 s)
1. 0–6 s: yesterday's number vs today's (the hook)
2. 6–20 s: comment shout-outs with literal, funny fulfilment
3. 20–35 s: vote results, lowest to highest, winner built
4. 35–48 s: milestone or mystery progress, more shout-outs, "N new houses"
5. 48–60 s: next vote (A/B/C), the next milestone tease, "Follow. See you tomorrow."

### How we stay different
- **Our identity:** the green population sign. "Population: You" literally counts viewers, and that's our signature. Theirs is the mayor.
- **Our tone:** the narrator *is* the city's first resident ("This city has one resident. Me.") and is outnumbered and slightly overwhelmed by the citizens. It's personal, not a mascot comedy.
- **Our own mechanics:** the Founders' Wall, the electricity milestone, a city name chosen by vote, and "your house" reply clips.

### Character ideas (pick one in Ep 2)
- **The narrator, shown as a small figure:** a little low-poly person with a hard hat, the "one resident" from Ep 1, now overwhelmed by neighbours.
- **A pigeon living on the population sign:** the real first resident, who judges every build.
- **A grumpy crane** that has to build everything the comments ask for.
